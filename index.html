About the university

Medical University – Pleven

Medical University Pleven is one of the five medical universities in Bulgaria. It was established in 1974, expanding on the traditions of Pleven City Hospital, founded in 1865.

Today it combines the traditions of the past with contemporary pre-clinical and clinical facilities — a University Hospital with more than 1,000 beds, modern diagnostic equipment, and specialised clinics and research units.

Education runs through three Faculties, a Medical College and a specialised Department: Faculty of Medicine, Faculty of Public Health, Faculty of Health Care, Medical College, and the Department of Language and Specialised Training.

6-year English Medicine programme Nursing & Midwifery (4 yrs) 30+ nationalities on campus
Teaching methodology

A Blended, Hands-On Learning Approach

The university implements a blended learning approach that progressively awards students greater autonomy as they progress through the programme, with a special focus on practical and small-group teaching.

This is supported by large-group lectures covering theoretical aspects of the programme, plus seminars and tutorials run individually or in small groups.

While many universities have moved away from full-body dissection, MU Pleven still offers unbeatable, hands-on anatomy training in its specialised dissection halls.

"Students are fully supported by professors and assistants — they are always available to help with any problems you have."

Trust & recognition

Accreditation & Recognition

Medical University Pleven is a self-governed, accredited state university — one of Bulgaria's leading medical institutions.

WHO World Health Organization listed
ECFMG Eligible for USMLE pathway
NMC National Medical Commission, India eligible
EQAR European Quality Assurance Register
ENQA European Assoc. for Quality Assurance in HE

European graduates are eligible to apply for full registration with regulatory bodies such as the General Medical Council (UK) in all EEA member states, and are also eligible to sit the USMLE and the Medical Council of India examinations.
